Four decisions, the same at every camp.

Not a setting. The shape of the thing.

01

No server, ever

Not as a privacy feature bolted on afterwards, but as the shape of the thing. There is nowhere to sign in because there is nowhere to sign in to. That means no outage can take your data away, no change of business model can start collecting it, and no breach can leak it.

02

It works with no signal

In a shop basement, on a building site, on a mountain at dawn. Recognition runs on the device — Apple's own on-device models — so the app is exactly as capable with the aeroplane mode switch on.

03

Nothing is guessed

Where a figure cannot be worked out honestly, these apps say so instead of showing a number. A confident wrong answer is worse than no answer, because it looks identical to a right one.

04

Free days first, then one clear price

Almost every app gives you a few days of everything before it asks for anything — three days, seven in Talkquote. After that, most are a single payment and nothing renews. Talkquote is a subscription, monthly or yearly, and so is Weddinglume for planners — and either way you get the whole app, not a tier of it.

Support

Every app has its own support and privacy page — pick one above. They all work the same way: no account, nothing collected, and a purchase that follows your Apple Account rather than one device.

Restoring something you already paid for

A purchase belongs to your Apple Account, not to one device. On a new phone, or after reinstalling, open the app and choose Restore on the purchase screen. Nothing is charged again.

Cancelling a subscription

Open the App Store, tap your picture at the top right, then Subscriptions. That is the only place a subscription can be stopped — this app cannot do it for you, because Apple takes the money. Cancelling stops the next payment and leaves the period you have already paid for running to its end. A one-time purchase never renews and has nothing to cancel.
