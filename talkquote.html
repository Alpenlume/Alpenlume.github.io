Step by step

How to use it

From standing in the client's kitchen to the quotation being in their inbox. Five steps, none of which needs a signal.

01Step 1 of 5

Put your business in once

Settings holds your name, address, logo, VAT number, currency, default tax rate and your own terms. It goes on every document from then on. Talkquote supplies no terms of its own: they are your contract with your client, and nothing to do with the app.

02Step 2 of 5

Dictate the job on site

Tap the microphone and talk normally. Quantities, units and prices are pulled out of the sentences; anything the parser had to infer is flagged so you can look at it. The recognition runs on the device — no signal needed and nothing uploaded.

03Step 3 of 5

Check the lines and the margin

Every line shows the sentence it came from, one tap away. Add your cost price and Talkquote shows the profit and the margin — inside the app only. Your client never sees a cost price, because a client seeing your cost price is a conversation nobody wants.

04Step 4 of 5

Approve the figures

Before anything can be exported, you confirm in as many words that you checked the totals. The date, the version and the totals at that moment are kept with the quotation. Any later edit clears it, so the record can never describe numbers that have since changed.

05Step 5 of 5

Send it, and know where it got to

Export as PDF or Word and either share it anywhere or send it straight from the app — the mail opens already addressed to your client, with the document attached and a covering note written in their language. Select several quotations and they go in one message. Sending marks them as sent, and the list starts counting the days.

Questions

Questions people ask

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Dictation, parsing, PDF and Word generation all run on the device. You can write a full quotation in a basement with no signal.

The only network traffic Talkquote causes belongs to the App Store, when you subscribe or restore.

Is the quotation legally binding?

That is between you and your client, and it depends on your own terms — which you write and Talkquote prints. The app supplies no terms of its own and makes no promise on your behalf.

What Talkquote does give you is a record: when you approved the figures, which version of the app was running, and what the totals were at that moment. If a figure is ever disputed, that is worth considerably more than any wording.

Can it get a price wrong?

It can mis-hear a word, like any recognition. That is why every line shows the sentence it came from, why anything inferred is flagged, and why nothing can be exported until you have confirmed the totals yourself. Talkquote drafts; you decide.

Can I send several quotations at once?

Yes. On the list, choose “Select several”, tick the ones you want and send — they go as attachments in one message.

If they are all for the same client the message is already addressed. If they are for different clients the To field is left empty on purpose: putting two clients on one e-mail would show each of them the other's address.

How do the seven free days work?

Talkquote can be used in full for seven days before it asks for anything. The days start by themselves when you first open the app: nothing is charged and nothing renews. When the days are up, the app asks once and you decide.

How is it paid for?

Fornela, Fornela Dolce and Talkquote are a subscription — monthly, or yearly for less. Melopocket, the three Sharpfield instruments and the three Stopwise apps are bought once and never renew. Either way you get the whole app. There are no tiers, no extra packs and nothing held back — and Apple takes the payment, so this developer never sees a card or a name.

Can I change my mind?

Yes. Cancel at any time in the App Store: tap your picture at the top right, then Subscriptions. That is the only place a subscription can be stopped — the app cannot do it for you, because Apple takes the money. You keep it until the end of the period you have already paid for.

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us — we never see your payment details and cannot issue one. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the purchase.

What happens on my other devices?

Whatever you bought belongs to your Apple Account. Sign in on an iPad or a new iPhone with the same Apple Account, open the app, and choose Restore on the purchase screen. Nothing is charged again.

One purchase covers one Apple Account. It is not shared with a family and cannot be passed round a firm — a second person needs their own.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. There is no account, no sign-in and no server. Everything the app holds is on your own device and is removed when you delete the app.

The only network traffic the app causes belongs to the App Store, when you buy, subscribe or restore.

Which languages does it use?

Whatever your device is set to, if that language is one it has been translated into; otherwise English. There is no language setting to get wrong — changing your iPhone's language changes the app with it.

Support

Payments, refunds and cancellations

Apple takes every payment for this app. That means Apple — and only Apple — can look a charge up, stop a subscription or give a refund. This developer never sees a card, a name or a payment, and has no way to change one.

Cancelling is done at the App Store, and nowhere else. Open the App Store, tap your picture at the top right, then Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next payment; what you have already paid for runs to the end of its period. Do it at least 24 hours before the period ends, or that renewal goes through anyway.

What Talkquote does not do

Talkquote drafts a quotation from what you said and prints it under your name. It does not check your prices, know your local rules, or take any responsibility for the figures. You approve every quotation before it can leave the app, and that approval is the point: the numbers are yours.

A word was recognised wrongly

Every line keeps the sentence it came from — tap the line to see it. Fix the number in place; the quotation's approval clears itself so you are asked to check the new total before it can be sent.

The export button is greyed out

Two things disable it: a quotation with no lines, and a quotation whose figures you have not approved since the last edit. Open the approval section and confirm the totals.

Nothing happens when I choose “Send by email”

That needs a mail account set up on the device. Add one in Settings, or use Share instead — that reaches Gmail, Outlook, WhatsApp and anything else you have installed.

Restoring something you already paid for

A purchase belongs to your Apple Account, not to one device. On a new phone, or after reinstalling, open the app and choose Restore on the purchase screen. Nothing is charged again.

Cancelling a subscription

Open the App Store, tap your picture at the top right, then Subscriptions. That is the only place a subscription can be stopped — this app cannot do it for you, because Apple takes the money. Cancelling stops the next payment and leaves the period you have already paid for running to its end. A one-time purchase never renews and has nothing to cancel.

Privacy policy

Last updated 10 September 2026.

The short version

Talkquote collects nothing, sends nothing and has no account. Your quotations and your clients' details stay on your device.

What stays on your device

Your company profile, your clients, your quotations and the documents you export live in the app's own storage on your device. There is no account and no server belonging to this app.

Dictation. Speech recognition runs on your device, using Apple's offline model, whenever your phone has that model for the language it is set to. When it has not, dictation stops and says so rather than quietly doing something else: it offers to send that one recording to Apple's speech service, under Apple's privacy policy, and nothing is sent unless you tap the button that says so. The answer is never remembered — the next dictation asks again — and you can always type instead.

The assistant. Talkquote can optionally ask an AI provider to help draft wording. It is off until you switch it on and enter your own key for a provider you chose. Then what you send goes from your phone straight to that provider, under your own account and their privacy policy. It never passes through the developer, and there is nothing here that could read it.

A document goes somewhere else only when you send or share it, and then only where you send it.

What is collected

Nothing. This app has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising identifier, no crash reporting service and no tracking of any kind. No profile is built and nothing is shared with anyone.

Network use

This app has no server of its own: nothing to sign in to, and nothing uploaded to the developer, ever. What it does with the internet — for most of these apps, nothing at all — is set out under “On your device” above.

Two things do use the internet, and both are somebody else's. The App Store does, when you buy, subscribe or restore. And when you share something — a dish, a recipe, a document — iOS hands it to the app you picked, whether that is Messages, Mail or WhatsApp, and that app sends it over its own connection under its own privacy policy. Nothing is sent unless you press send, and this app never learns where it went.

Purchases

Buying is handled entirely by Apple. The app never sees a card number, a name or an address; it asks the App Store one question — whether this Apple Account owns the app — and receives yes or no. Apple's own privacy policy governs the transaction.

Children

This app is not directed at children and collects nothing from anybody, so there is nothing held about a child to disclose, correct or delete.

Your rights

Data protection law gives you the right to see, correct, export and erase personal data held about you. Because none is held, there is nothing to produce or delete on request. Everything the app has is on your own device: deleting the app removes it, and it was never anywhere else.

Changes

If this policy ever changes it will be dated above. It will not be changed retroactively, because there is no collected data for a change to newly permit.
