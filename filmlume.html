FilmlumeFilm negatives, into photos

Your film negatives, turned into real photos.

Photograph a negative on a light — an iPad showing white is enough — and bring the picture in from Photos or Files. Filmlume reads colour negative, black-and-white and slide the way a lab scanner does, in density with the orange mask divided out, and gives the picture back at the full resolution it came in. And any photo already on your phone can be printed on one of 37 film looks.

01Positive at once

The picture, the moment it is in.

Bring in a photo of a negative and the positive is on screen at once — balanced on its own greys, the orange mask gone. Hold to see the negative again, star the good ones, and use one frame’s edit on the whole roll.

02The film you shot

Printed in its own character.

Pick the look of the film you shot — 37 of our own, from soft portrait colour and vivid daylight to gritty black-and-white and bright slide film — and print the photos already on your phone on them too. Every picture stays editable.

03A light hand

The few things a printer changes.

Exposure, contrast, highlights, shadows, warmth, tint, saturation and a colour head’s filtration, with a live histogram, pinch to zoom, a straighten and a crop — every figure moves the moment the slider does, a double tap puts any of them back to zero, and a swipe takes you straight to the next frame. Take the film base from the rebate, or tap something grey to balance the colour exactly.

How to use it

From a strip of film to a picture on your phone.

1Step 1 of 5

Light it

Lay the negative on a light table — or on an iPad showing the app’s own light table.

2Step 2 of 5

Photograph it

Square over the film, with any camera or your phone’s own Camera app. RAW, DNG or TIFF keeps the most.

3Step 3 of 5

Bring the frames in

Start a roll — the film and the format — and pick the pictures in Photos or Files.

4Step 4 of 5

Adjust

Film look, light, colour and crop — non-destructive, always undoable.

5Step 5 of 5

Save or share

Every pixel of the original to Photos as 10-bit HEIF, a JPEG to send, or a 16-bit TIFF to wherever you edit. Only a crop makes it smaller.

Payments, refunds and cancellations

Apple takes every payment for this app. That means Apple — and only Apple — can look a charge up, stop a subscription or give a refund. This developer never sees a card, a name or a payment, and has no way to change one.

Cancelling is done at the App Store, and nowhere else. Open the App Store, tap your picture at the top right, then Subscriptions. What this app does not do

It does not remove dust and scratches by itself, and it is not a drum scanner. Colour is an interpretation, as it is in every scan; the film looks are starting points, not certified emulations, and every picture can be adjusted.

Restoring something you already paid for

Privacy policy

Last updated 10 September 2026.

The short version

Filmlume collects nothing and sends nothing. Your pictures stay on your device.

What stays on your device

The photographs of your negatives, your rolls and every edit are files in the app on your device. There is no server and no account. The app has no camera of its own: you choose pictures in the system pickers, and they leave the app only when you save or share them.

What is collected

Nothing. This app has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising identifier, no crash reporting service and no tracking of any kind. No profile is built and nothing is shared with anyone.

Network use

This app has no server of its own: nothing to sign in to, and nothing uploaded to the developer, ever. What it does with the internet — for most of these apps, nothing at all — is set out under “On your device” above.

Two things do use the internet, and both are somebody else's. The App Store does, when you buy, subscribe or restore. And when you share something — a dish, a recipe, a document — iOS hands it to the app you picked, whether that is Messages, Mail or WhatsApp, and that app sends it over its own connection under its own privacy policy. Nothing is sent unless you press send, and this app never learns where it went.

Purchases

Buying is handled entirely by Apple. The app never sees a card number, a name or an address; it asks the App Store one question — whether this Apple Account owns the app — and receives yes or no. Apple's own privacy policy governs the transaction.

Children

This app is not directed at children and collects nothing from anybody, so there is nothing held about a child to disclose, correct or delete.

Your rights

Data protection law gives you the right to see, correct, export and erase personal data held about you. Because none is held, there is nothing to produce or delete on request. Everything the app has is on your own device: deleting the app removes it, and it was never anywhere else.

Changes

If this policy ever changes it will be dated above. It will not be changed retroactively, because there is no collected data for a change to newly permit.
