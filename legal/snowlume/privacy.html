Who is responsible

Snowlume is published by Alpenlume, the name under which Mariusz Maliszewski — a private individual resident in Switzerland, trading as a sole proprietor — publishes apps (“we”, “the developer”). Mariusz Maliszewski is the controller for the purposes of this policy.

For anything to do with your privacy, write to alpenlume@gmail.com; we answer every message ourselves. Our full postal address is given on request, and is shown with the app on the App Store in the European Union.

What we collect

Nothing. Snowlume has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no advertising identifier, no crash-reporting service and no tracking of any kind. We run no server for the app. We do not build profiles, and we do not sell, rent or share personal information — we do not have any.

The App Store's “App Privacy” label for Snowlume is therefore “Data Not Collected”.

What stays on your device

The information you put into Snowlume is stored only in the app's own storage on your iPhone or iPad, protected by iOS encryption and your passcode. That includes:

  • the people on your gift list, their budgets and their gifts, with what each cost and how far along it is
  • your Christmas cards and their words
  • letters to Santa, with the first name, age, good deeds and wishes written in them
  • which Christmas the countdown counts to, the name you sign with, and your other settings

If your device backs up to iCloud or a computer, that backup is made by Apple's iOS under your settings and Apple's privacy policy; we never see it. Deleting the app deletes everything it stored.

Gift ideas

The gift helper uses only what you choose or type in it — an age group, interests and a few words. On devices with Apple Intelligence, the ideas are suggested by Apple's language model on your device; elsewhere they are taken from ideas written into the app. What you type there is not sent to the developer, to Apple or to anybody else.

Motion

The tilt of the phone moves the snow and the layers of a card's picture. The motion sensors are read on the device while those screens are open, and nothing is recorded.

Photos

When you choose to save a card or a letter as a picture, the app adds it to your photo library. It asks for add-only access and cannot see or read the photos already there.

Printing and PDF

When you print, iOS sends the pages to the AirPrint printer you pick. A PDF is made on your device and goes only where you save or send it.

AirDrop, Messages and Mail

When you send a card or a letter, its pictures and PDF go to the person or app you choose in the share sheet, or by AirDrop to the iPhone you hold yours near. Nothing is sent unless you send it.

Permissions

Snowlume asks for a permission only at the moment a feature needs it, says why, and works without it where it can. You can change any of them at any time in Settings › Snowlume.

  • Photos (add only, when you save a picture)

Apple, Siri and purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. Snowlume never sees your name, card or address; it asks the App Store only whether this Apple Account owns the app or a subscription. Apple's privacy policy (apple.com/legal/privacy) governs the transaction.

Siri, Shortcuts and Spotlight. If you use Snowlume's actions with Siri or the Shortcuts app, your request is processed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and the app receives only the action and the values it needs to carry it out.

Sharing. When you share something from the app, iOS hands it to the app you pick, which sends it under its own privacy policy. Nothing is sent unless you send it, and we never learn where it went.

If you write to us

If you email alpenlume@gmail.com, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach, and use them only to answer you. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering, or the performance of our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(f) and (b) GDPR; Art. 31 FADP). Messages are kept for as long as the conversation needs and deleted within 24 months after it ends, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

Our email is provided by Google (Gmail), which processes messages on our behalf and may do so in the United States and other countries, under Google's safeguards for international transfers, including standard contractual clauses. We share support messages with nobody else.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us what personal data we hold about you (usually none), and have it corrected, deleted or handed to you. In particular:

  • Switzerland (FADP): the rights to information, correction, deletion and data portability, and to complain to the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC, edoeb.admin.ch).
  • EU and UK (GDPR): the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, to withdraw consent, and to complain to the supervisory authority where you live or work.
  • California (CCPA/CPRA): the rights to know, delete and correct personal information, to opt out of its sale or sharing, and not to be discriminated against for using these rights. We have not sold or shared personal information in the past 12 months, do not use sensitive personal information, and have collected none except what you send by email, which is used only to answer you.

To use any of these rights, email alpenlume@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days and never charge for it.

Children

Snowlume is made for adults. Its letter to Santa is meant to be filled in by a child together with a parent or another grown-up, on the grown-up's device. The app collects nothing from anybody: a child's first name, age and wishes written in a letter stay in the app on that device, and go only where the grown-up prints or sends the letter. There is no chat, no account and no contact with anybody through the app. If you believe a child has sent us personal data by email, write to us and we will delete it.

Security and automated decisions

Because nothing leaves your device, your information is protected by your device's own security: its passcode, Face ID or Touch ID, and iOS encryption. Snowlume makes no automated decisions about you and does no profiling.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date, and a significant change will be noted in the app's release notes. A change will never permit collecting data retroactively.
