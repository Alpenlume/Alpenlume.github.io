How to use it

From the day you set the date to the morning the caterer needs the final numbers. Nothing here needs a signal, and nothing asks you to sign in.

Questions

Questions people ask

Do guests reply inside the app?

No. They reply the way they already do — a message, a card, a word at the door — and you write it down. Replying in the app would need an account for every guest and a server to hold it, and then the guest list would be somebody's database.

What the app does instead is count properly: answers in seats, so a guest with a plus-one and two children is four chairs on the caterer's number.

Does it work for a wedding that is not a Western one?

Yes, and that is the point of the program. A tradition — Japanese, Chinese, South Asian, Middle Eastern, Western, or one you build yourself — offers the events it usually has and switches on the parts of a wedding it needs, such as money gifts instead of a registry.

None of it is fixed. Every event can be renamed, moved or deleted, and the app never tells anybody how to hold a wedding or what an amount should be.

We are paying in two currencies. Does it convert?

No, deliberately. A conversion needs an exchange rate, a rate needs a server, and the number would be wrong by the next morning — which is worse than no number when it is a deposit you are checking.

Each wedding is planned in one currency and each gift keeps its own. A planner's dashboard adds each currency up on its own and shows them side by side.

I plan weddings for a living. Is there a per-seat price?

No. One subscription covers every client you have, with three days free first. There are no seats, no tiers and nothing held back.

It is a subscription rather than one payment because a planner works all year, and because an agency's data is other people's — which is exactly why none of it touches a server.

Can I move a wedding to another country?

Yes. Change the venue's time zone and the wall clock stays put: a wedding at three in the afternoon is still at three in the afternoon there, rather than becoming whatever three o'clock here turns into.

How is it paid for?

Three days free, with nothing charged and nothing to cancel. After that a couple pays once and it is theirs — nothing renews. A planner subscribes, monthly or by the year, and can cancel any time in Settings on the iPhone.

Apple takes the payment, so this developer never sees a card or a name.

What is collected about me?

Nothing at all. There is no account, no analytics and no network call to any server of ours. The app works with the aeroplane mode on.

Who makes it?

Alpenlume, in Switzerland. Support is one e-mail address and a person who answers it.

Support

Payments, refunds and cancellations

Apple takes every payment for this app. That means Apple — and only Apple — can look a charge up, stop a subscription or give a refund. This developer never sees a card, a name or a payment, and has no way to change one.

Cancelling is done at the App Store, and nowhere else. Open the App Store, tap your picture at the top right, then Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next payment; what you have already paid for runs to the end of its period. Do it at least 24 hours before the period ends, or that renewal goes through anyway.

What Weddinglume does not do

It does not collect replies from your guests, book anything, take a payment, or recommend a vendor to you. It holds what you and the people helping you have decided, adds it up correctly, and prints it. It gives no advice about money and none about how a wedding should be held.

Restoring something you already paid for

A purchase belongs to your Apple Account, not to one device. On a new phone, or after reinstalling, open the app and choose Restore on the purchase screen. Nothing is charged again.

Cancelling a subscription

Open the App Store, tap your picture at the top right, then Subscriptions. That is the only place a subscription can be stopped — this app cannot do it for you, because Apple takes the money. Cancelling stops the next payment and leaves the period you have already paid for running to its end. A one-time purchase never renews and has nothing to cancel.

Privacy policy

Last updated 10 September 2026.

The short version

Weddinglume collects nothing, sends nothing and has no account. Your guest list, your budget and your clients' details stay on your device.

What stays on your device

Your weddings, your guests, your budget, your vendors and every plan you print live in the app's own storage on your device. There is no account, no server and nothing to sign into, so there is no copy of your guest list anywhere else — and, for a planner, no copy of a client's.

What is collected

Nothing. This app has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising identifier, no crash reporting service and no tracking of any kind. No profile is built and nothing is shared with anyone.

Network use

This app has no server of its own: nothing to sign in to, and nothing uploaded to the developer, ever. What it does with the internet — for most of these apps, nothing at all — is set out under “On your device” above.

Two things do use the internet, and both are somebody else's. The App Store does, when you buy, subscribe or restore. And when you share something — a dish, a recipe, a document — iOS hands it to the app you picked, whether that is Messages, Mail or WhatsApp, and that app sends it over its own connection under its own privacy policy. Nothing is sent unless you press send, and this app never learns where it went.

Purchases

Buying is handled entirely by Apple. The app never sees a card number, a name or an address; it asks the App Store one question — whether this Apple Account owns the app — and receives yes or no. Apple's own privacy policy governs the transaction.

Children

This app is not directed at children and collects nothing from anybody, so there is nothing held about a child to disclose, correct or delete.

Your rights

Data protection law gives you the right to see, correct, export and erase personal data held about you. Because none is held, there is nothing to produce or delete on request. Everything the app has is on your own device: deleting the app removes it, and it was never anywhere else.

Changes

If this policy ever changes it will be dated above. It will not be changed retroactively, because there is no collected data for a change to newly permit.
