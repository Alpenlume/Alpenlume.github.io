Step by step

How to use it

The first letter, in the order you would write it. None of it needs an account, and none of it needs a signal.

01Step 1 of 5

Start a letter

Tap the plus. Write the greeting, then the words, then how you sign — or set your name once in Settings and every letter starts signed.

02Step 2 of 5

Let the assistant begin, if you like

Choose a tone, give it a few words, and take its draft into your letter — or just the lines you want.

03Step 3 of 5

Choose the paper and the hand

Eight papers and five handwritings, changed with a tap while you watch the page.

04Step 4 of 5

Add your photographs

Up to three, each in its own frame — an instant photo, a heart, an oval, a circle or a stamp.

05Step 5 of 5

Send it, or print it

AirDrop, Messages or Mail send a picture and a PDF; Save to Photos keeps a copy; Print gives you the page.

Questions

Questions people ask

Does the assistant write the letter for me?

It writes a first draft from the words you give it, in the tone you choose — and nothing more. It is told to invent nothing about the two of you: no places, dates or memories of its own. You read it, change it, and sign it.

Is what I write sent anywhere?

No. Your letters, cards and photographs stay in the app on your device, and the assistant works on the device too. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself.

What does the other person receive?

A picture of each page, which opens straight into Photos on an iPhone, and a PDF for printing. They do not need Lovelume.

Can I print on ordinary paper?

Yes — on any AirPrint printer. A letter prints on one page; a card as its front and its inside. Colours depend on your printer and paper.

Where do the flowers come from?

They are drawn by the app for each card — a bouquet of the kind and colours you chose, arranged a little differently every time you ask.

How is it paid for?

Everything is open for your first three days, with a free in-app trial. After that, one in-app purchase unlocks Lovelume for good. It never renews and there is nothing to cancel.

Apple takes the payment, so this developer never sees a card or a name.

What happens on my other devices?

The purchase belongs to your Apple Account. On another iPhone or iPad signed in to it, open Lovelume and choose Restore purchase in Settings. Nothing is charged again.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us — we never see your payment details. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the purchase.

Support

Payments, refunds and cancellations

Apple takes every payment for this app. That means Apple — and only Apple — can look a charge up, stop a subscription or give a refund. This developer never sees a card, a name or a payment, and has no way to change one.

Cancelling is done at the App Store, and nowhere else. Open the App Store, tap your picture at the top right, then Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next payment; what you have already paid for runs to the end of its period. Do it at least 24 hours before the period ends, or that renewal goes through anyway.

Saving asks for Photos

Lovelume asks only to add to your photo library, never to read it. If you declined, allow it in Settings › Lovelume › Photos › Add Photos Only.

Holding the iPhones together does nothing

That way of starting AirDrop is iOS's own, and it needs AirDrop's “Bringing Devices Together” switched on (Settings › General › AirDrop) on both iPhones. The Send button always works.

The assistant writes the same few sentences

Where Apple Intelligence is not available or not turned on, the draft is put together from sentences written for each tone, with your words in the middle. Turn on Apple Intelligence in Settings on a device that supports it for drafts of its own.

The printer is not listed

Lovelume prints with AirPrint. Make sure the printer is on the same Wi‑Fi network as your iPhone.

Restoring something you already paid for

A purchase belongs to your Apple Account, not to one device. On a new phone, or after reinstalling, open the app and choose Restore on the purchase screen. Nothing is charged again.

Cancelling a subscription

Open the App Store, tap your picture at the top right, then Subscriptions. That is the only place a subscription can be stopped — this app cannot do it for you, because Apple takes the money. Cancelling stops the next payment and leaves the period you have already paid for running to its end. A one-time purchase never renews and has nothing to cancel.

Privacy policy

Last updated 10 September 2026.

The short version

Lovelume collects nothing and has no account. Your letters, cards and photographs stay on your device.

What stays on your device

Your letters, the photographs in them and your cards live in the app's own storage on your device. You choose photographs in iOS's own picker, so the app sees only the ones you pick; saving adds to Photos and cannot read what is there. The writing assistant works on the device and sends nothing anywhere.

What is collected

Nothing. This app has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising identifier, no crash reporting service and no tracking of any kind. No profile is built and nothing is shared with anyone.

Network use

This app has no server of its own: nothing to sign in to, and nothing uploaded to the developer, ever. What it does with the internet — for most of these apps, nothing at all — is set out under “On your device” above.

Two things do use the internet, and both are somebody else's. The App Store does, when you buy, subscribe or restore. And when you share something — a dish, a recipe, a document — iOS hands it to the app you picked, whether that is Messages, Mail or WhatsApp, and that app sends it over its own connection under its own privacy policy. Nothing is sent unless you press send, and this app never learns where it went.

Purchases

Buying is handled entirely by Apple. The app never sees a card number, a name or an address; it asks the App Store one question — whether this Apple Account owns the app — and receives yes or no. Apple's own privacy policy governs the transaction.

Children

This app is not directed at children and collects nothing from anybody, so there is nothing held about a child to disclose, correct or delete.

Your rights

Data protection law gives you the right to see, correct, export and erase personal data held about you. Because none is held, there is nothing to produce or delete on request. Everything the app has is on your own device: deleting the app removes it, and it was never anywhere else.

Changes

If this policy ever changes it will be dated above. It will not be changed retroactively, because there is no collected data for a change to newly permit.
