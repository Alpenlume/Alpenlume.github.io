Step by step

How to use it

The first evening, in the order you would do it. None of it needs an account, and none of it needs a signal.

01Step 1 of 6

Find one you like

Discover opens on the Minimal collection; filter by 3D, Live or Still, or by minimal, gradients, glass, silk, light, landscapes or the Melopocket collection.

02Step 2 of 6

Touch it

Open a wallpaper and put a finger on it: it ripples and swirls, then settles. Tilt the phone to see the 3D ones in depth.

03Step 3 of 6

Make it yours

On a glass wallpaper, tune how the glass bends, splits the light, frosts and shines.

04Step 4 of 6

See it as your Lock Screen

Switch between the clean picture, a sketch of the Lock Screen and one of the Home Screen, so you see it with the time and the icons on it.

05Step 5 of 6

Save it at the right size

This screen, 4K, iPad, or either screen of iPhone Duo — each drawn afresh at that size. Moving wallpapers save as Live Photos.

06Step 6 of 6

A new one every morning

In Shortcuts, make an automation with “Next Glasslume Wallpaper” and “Set Wallpaper”. Glasslume keeps a week of pictures ready, favourites first.

Questions

Questions people ask

Are the wallpapers downloaded?

No. Each one is drawn on your device by the app's own graphics code — the same picture on every device, at whatever size you ask for. Nothing is downloaded and nothing is uploaded.

Does my Lock Screen ripple when I touch it?

The ripples, the swirls and the tilt play inside Glasslume. iOS lets an app give you a picture or a Live Photo for your Lock Screen, not a program — so the wallpaper you save is the picture you chose, sharp at your screen's size. A Live Photo moves when iOS plays it.

Who designed the minimal ones?

They are original designs drawn by the app itself — a few flat shapes in five colours, placed with care, with a little paper grain — not copies of any poster or artwork.

How does the new wallpaper every morning work?

Shortcuts can set your wallpaper; Glasslume gives it the picture. Make an automation (every morning at seven, say) with two actions: “Next Glasslume Wallpaper”, then “Set Wallpaper”. Glasslume keeps seven pictures ready at your screen's size and tops them up whenever you open it.

Does it work on iPhone Duo?

Every screen of the app adapts to the width it is given, folded or open. Wallpapers can be saved at the size of either iPhone Duo screen, and at the exact size of whatever screen you are holding.

How is it paid for?

Wallpapers No. 1 to No. 8 — minimal ones, stills, Live and 3D — save without paying anything. One in-app purchase unlocks the other three hundred and ninety-two. It never renews and there is nothing to cancel.

Apple takes the payment, so this developer never sees a card or a name.

What happens on my other devices?

The purchase belongs to your Apple Account. On another iPhone or iPad signed in to it, open Glasslume and choose Restore purchase in Settings. Nothing is charged again.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us — we never see your payment details. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the purchase.

Support

Payments, refunds and cancellations

Apple takes every payment for this app. That means Apple — and only Apple — can look a charge up, stop a subscription or give a refund. This developer never sees a card, a name or a payment, and has no way to change one.

Cancelling is done at the App Store, and nowhere else. Open the App Store, tap your picture at the top right, then Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next payment; what you have already paid for runs to the end of its period. Do it at least 24 hours before the period ends, or that renewal goes through anyway.

Saving asks for Photos

Glasslume asks only to add to your photo library, never to read it. If you declined, allow it in Settings › Glasslume › Photos › Add Photos Only.

Setting a Live Photo as the Lock Screen

Long-press the Lock Screen, tap the plus, choose Photos and pick the Live Photo. iOS decides whether a Live Photo can move on the Lock Screen of a given device; if it cannot, it is shown as the still from its middle.

“Next Glasslume Wallpaper” says nothing is ready

The pictures are drawn while the app is open, because iOS does not let an app use the graphics chip in the background. Open Glasslume once and it prepares the next seven.

Restoring something you already paid for

A purchase belongs to your Apple Account, not to one device. On a new phone, or after reinstalling, open the app and choose Restore on the purchase screen. Nothing is charged again.

Cancelling a subscription

Open the App Store, tap your picture at the top right, then Subscriptions. That is the only place a subscription can be stopped — this app cannot do it for you, because Apple takes the money. Cancelling stops the next payment and leaves the period you have already paid for running to its end. A one-time purchase never renews and has nothing to cancel.

Still stuck? Write to alpenlume@gmail.com — say which app, which iPhone or iPad, and what you expected to happen.

Privacy policy

Last updated 10 September 2026.

The short version

Glasslume collects nothing and has no account. The wallpapers are drawn on your device; what you choose stays there.

What stays on your device

Your favourites and the look of the app live in the app's own storage on your device. Saving adds a picture to Photos and cannot read what is there.

What is collected

Nothing. This app has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising identifier, no crash reporting service and no tracking of any kind. No profile is built and nothing is shared with anyone.

Network use

This app has no server of its own: nothing to sign in to, and nothing uploaded to the developer, ever. What it does with the internet — for most of these apps, nothing at all — is set out under “On your device” above.

Two things do use the internet, and both are somebody else's. The App Store does, when you buy, subscribe or restore. And when you share something — a dish, a recipe, a document — iOS hands it to the app you picked, whether that is Messages, Mail or WhatsApp, and that app sends it over its own connection under its own privacy policy. Nothing is sent unless you press send, and this app never learns where it went.

Purchases

Buying is handled entirely by Apple. The app never sees a card number, a name or an address; it asks the App Store one question — whether this Apple Account owns the app — and receives yes or no. Apple's own privacy policy governs the transaction.

Children

This app is not directed at children and collects nothing from anybody, so there is nothing held about a child to disclose, correct or delete.

Your rights

Data protection law gives you the right to see, correct, export and erase personal data held about you. Because none is held, there is nothing to produce or delete on request. Everything the app has is on your own device: deleting the app removes it, and it was never anywhere else.

Changes

If this policy ever changes it will be dated above. It will not be changed retroactively, because there is no collected data for a change to newly permit.
