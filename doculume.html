Questions

Questions

Is a drawn signature a legal, certified signature?

No. It marks a document the way a pen does, and DocuLume Pro never says otherwise. Check what the other side to an agreement, or the law that applies to it, requires before you rely on one.

Does it open the files other office programs make?

Yes — .docx and .pdf are open, standard formats that many programs read and write, not files that need one particular program to open. DocuLume Pro is not made or endorsed by the companies behind any other program that also reads them.

What happens after the three free days?

Nothing is charged automatically. Every file still opens, reads, prints and shares. Writing, marking up, signing and scanning ask once for the one-time unlock, shown at the App Store’s own price.

Where do my files go?

Into this app’s own folder on this iPhone, in ordinary .docx, .pdf, .rtf and .txt files — visible in the Files app, importable and exportable through iOS’s own file picker and share sheet. There is no account and no server.
